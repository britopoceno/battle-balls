Unowned QA gate findings go into the owning story as a new AC, with a version bump and a Change Log row. No routing note goes back into the source gate or story. Precedent: cf9402b (QA-D8-01 attached to e4.2) touched only e4.2, and QA Results is @qa-only per story-lifecycle.

Where the e4.0 gate findings landed (commit 9f06e60, 2026-09-21):
- E40-SEC-001 went to e4.3 AC 16: `parseDoCliente` in `src/net/codec.ts`, plus a d.jogador-to-seat check on decisions.
- e4.4 got AC 15, which says it only *uses* the parser. Its AC 13 forbids `src/net/`.
- E40-REQ-002 went to e4.2 v1.3.0.
- E40-TST-003 set a canonical purity grep for e4.2, e4.3 and e4.4: `grep -rnwE` plus a separate `Date.now`/`performance.now` check, both required to exit 1.

**Why:** e4.4 is blocked on R-05 and cannot own files in net/. e4.3 is pure and covered by sim:check.

Attach-to-existing-story works only if that story **already opens the file**. When no Ready story owns the file, the finding needs a new story from @sm. The routing decision is then recorded in the source story's Change Log (any agent may append). Case: E41-TEL-002 (2026-09-21, commit 60b3206) needed a new story, suggested `debt.10` (telemetry must mark input delay; @architect owns the shape). No Ready story opens client/ or tools/telemetria.ts, and e4.5 forbids them. The debt.9 precondition (b) question ("x6.0 AND delay 6" vs mixed data) is open with the user/@pm.

Story ACs that count diff lines are fragile. e4.2 AC 6 went from "0 lines" to "1 line" to "annotation-only, no body lines" (v1.4.0). Specify the *nature* of the allowed lines and reproduce tsc on a scratch copy of src/ before fixing a count.

Parallel stories share a single working tree, so there are no worktrees. A story that runs alongside another in-flight story must say "start after the other story's implementation commit". Its scope check must be `git show --stat <own commit>`, never `git diff --stat` on the tree. A before/after `sim:check` would otherwise absorb the other story's new guards. An "X must not change" AC binds only the story that carries it. Precedent: debt.10 v1.1 (2026-09-21, commit 49dea57), sequenced after e4.2.
